Target × Airtable

 

Target is running its largest store transformation in a decade: 130-plus remodels and 30 new stores in 2026, each involving 400-plus contractors and year-long timelines. When project data lives in 37-tab spreadsheets, monthly business reviews take 80 hours and scope creep runs unchecked. Airtable connects planning, approvals, contractors, and executive visibility into one real-time view across every store project.

How Airtable connects your store project portfolio

Unified project portfolio
Every remodel, new store, and special project in one connected system. Real-time status, budget, and risk across the entire portfolio, replacing 37-tab spreadsheets and disconnected SharePoint sites.
Contractor coordination
Standardize how general contractors, specialty subcontractors, and internal teams hand off work. Track change orders, material deliveries, and milestones without rebuilding reports manually every month.
Executive visibility
Leadership sees status, spend, and risk across every store project at a glance. Monthly business reviews go from 80 hours of slide preparation to a live dashboard that updates itself.
Sits above construction tools
Airtable does not replace Procore or your construction management stack. It connects them to planning, approvals, merchandising, and executive reporting so day-to-day execution links to business outcomes.
