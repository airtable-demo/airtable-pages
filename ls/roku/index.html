Roku × Airtable

 

With Curate live and the Roku Ads API now open to platform partners, the number of integrations moving at once has outgrown what a launch deck can hold. DPG Media Group and DraftKings ran into the same wall and moved partner and launch coordination onto Airtable, where owners, stages, and dependencies stay current without a status chase. It is the same operating pattern the campaign teams at Roku already run on Airtable, pointed at the ad platform pipeline.

How Airtable changes the way the ad platform teams operate

One pipeline for every partner
Data partners, measurement vendors, and demand platforms live in one place, each with an owner, a stage, and a live go-to-market date instead of a slide that ages the moment it is shared.
Certification and beta gates that hold
Technical certification, beta cohort, and contract steps become tracked gates rather than side conversations, so nothing reaches a launch date with an open dependency nobody flagged.
Rollups leadership can read
Portfolio views roll up automatically across partnerships, ad product, and revenue operations, which retires the weekly status collection cycle rather than adding another report to it.
Complements the systems already in place
It sits alongside the CRM, the ticketing system, and the data warehouse, connecting the coordination layer between them rather than replacing any of them.

Teams involved
